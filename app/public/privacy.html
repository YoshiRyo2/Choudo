Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

In short: Choudo has no accounts. What you enter stays on your phone (or, on the website, in your browser) and is never sent anywhere. When you check a product link, only that product’s code goes out, to fetch its size chart. The iPhone app collects nothing; the website counts anonymous visits.

Who we are

Choudo is a small app made by two people. It shows how each size of a garment would fit you, from the brand’s size chart and your measurements. You can reach us at Choudo.app@gmail.com.

What the app keeps, and where

To work out how clothes fit, the app keeps what you give it:

  • your height, weight, body type and any measurements you add (chest or bust, waist, hips, inseam, bra size);
  • your fit reference (a tee you like, or a style), and your units;
  • the garments you like or pass, your folders and saved outfits, and what’s on the mannequin;
  • the products you’ve checked from a link (up to 30: their size chart, name, price, colours and photo addresses), so checking one again doesn’t ask the brand again.

All of it is stored on your iPhone (and in your iPhone’s own backups, if you back it up to iCloud or a computer); on the website, in your browser only. There is no account and no sign-in. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we can’t see any of it.

What we don’t do

  • The iPhone app has no analytics or crash reporting, and nothing tracks you across apps or websites.
  • No ads.
  • We never sell or share data: we don’t have any to share.

Sharing an outfit

When you choose to share a saved outfit, the app makes a picture of it and hands it to the iPhone’s share sheet; you pick where it goes. The picture shows the mascot figure, the outfit’s name and each garment with its size. It never shows your height, weight or measurements in words. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Checking a product link

When you paste a product link and check it, Choudo reads the product’s code from the link (for example E424873-000) and asks the brand for that product’s size chart and details. Only the product code is sent (for Zara, the number in the link, for example 04174639, and the product code of the colour you’re looking at): never your height, weight, measurements, sizes or anything else you’ve entered. Today this works with Uniqlo US and Zara US.

  • The iPhone app asks Uniqlo or Zara directly. If Zara turns the app’s request away, the app asks Choudo’s fetch service (below) for that one product instead.
  • The website can’t ask the brand directly, so it asks Choudo’s fetch service, a small program we run on Cloudflare, which asks Uniqlo or Zara for that one product and passes the answer back. It keeps no logs of what you check, and keeps the brand’s answer for up to a day so the next person checking the same product gets it faster. Cloudflare may keep standard network logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.
  • The product’s photo is shown as the brand publishes it and loads from the brand’s image server (Uniqlo’s or Zara’s), so the brand sees that request as it would on its own site.

What Uniqlo or Zara does with a request is covered by that brand’s own privacy policy, not this one. On the website, we count that a check happened (not which link or product), with the anonymous counts described below.

Links to brands

“View the chart”, “Open at Uniqlo” and “Open at Zara” open the brand’s own page (its size chart, or the product) in a Safari window inside the app (on the website, in a new tab). What happens there is covered by that site’s privacy policy, not this one.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website (not the iPhone app) counts anonymous visits, and which screens are opened, with GoatCounter. No cookies, and never your measurements, sizes or the clothes you pick. GoatCounter handles those counts under its own privacy policy. These pages (privacy and support) aren’t counted.

Children

Choudo is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (on the website: clear the site’s data in your browser). That removes everything Choudo kept on your device. A copy stays in any iPhone backup made before, until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
