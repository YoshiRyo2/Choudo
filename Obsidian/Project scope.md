### Scope: Measurement-based outfit fit app (v1)

**What it does:** Shows how garments fit a user’s body, using measurements the user enters, and lets them compare sizes and build outfits.

**In scope for v1:**

- One category: crew-neck tees
- Three brands: Uniqlo, plus **[decide: second brand]** and **[decide: third brand]**
- Seven body measurements: height, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width, sleeve length, inseam
- Guest mode only, with no accounts, and data stored on the device
- Quick start (height and chest) and a shirt-based alternative

**Out of scope for v1:** Photos, camera or AR, face data, accounts, photoreal rendering, scraping, and any category besides tees.

**Success criteria:**

- Predictions match ten testers’ real fits at least ==80%== of the time for one brand and one category.
- Ten people say they’d use it again for a real purchase.

**Timeline:** About 12 weeks, part-time.
